Import sales

Sales come in two ways. With Claude: download the import guide, give it to Claude with the weekend's sales in whatever form they're in (Clover or Square exports, a sheet, a tally, photos), and import the JSON file Claude writes. It can hold each order and its drinks, or just the drinks sold per day, with or without dates. Directly: drop in Clover transactions, the Clover item sales report, or a sheet with one drink per row. Either way you check the preview before anything is saved.
